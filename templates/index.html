How your plan adapts

Start with 50% for Needs, 30% for Wants and 20% for Savings. If essential spending exceeds its allocation, available Wants funds move to Needs. Already-spent Wants funds and your Savings target stay protected. Pacing alerts estimate month-end expenses from your average daily spending.
